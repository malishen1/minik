Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Minik lets two people raise a shared virtual baby. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What is stored

The shared baby (its name, needs, progress), the first names you type for yourself and your partner, and the short notes you send each other are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) under a random 6-letter pairing code, so both phones can see the same baby. Anyone who knows the code can open that baby, so share it only with your partner. Nothing else is collected.

On your device

Your name and the pairing code are also kept on your iPhone. Reminders are scheduled locally.

Deleting

Delete the app on both phones to stop using the baby. To have the shared record removed from iCloud, open a request below with your code.

Children

Minik is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
